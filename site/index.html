download for macos

macos 14+, free, needs accessibility access

copy stuff like normal. hold cmdv and a small window shows up next to whatever you're typing in. click an item and it pastes. type to filter if you've copied too much.

how it works

  1. copy like you always do
  2. hold cmdv, the window opens by your cursor
  3. click something to paste it, or type to narrow things down

what it keeps

  • text, links, code, files, colors, images, rich text
  • search: filter as you type. the history window searches everything, even by meaning.
  • gather: tap cmd twice to combine a bunch of copies into one paste
  • drag and drop: drag a clip straight into any app
  • images: thumbnails with a hover preview, and you can pull text out of screenshots
  • transforms: paste as plain text, uppercase, json, base64, and more
  • pins and queue: pin the stuff you reuse, or queue a few to paste one at a time

privacy

  • everything is encrypted on disk, and the key stays on your mac
  • local only, no account, no cloud, nothing leaves the machine
  • passwords get spotted and hidden. you can exclude apps and let old stuff expire.

accessibility

respects reduced motion, reduced transparency, and larger system text sizes.
